The JAYRAJI Standard

Seed-to-Bottle Integrity

Unlike chemical industrial refining that heats oil above 200°C, JAYRAJI GROUP preserves the seed through slow mechanical crushing under 45°C.

Step 01

Local Seed Grading

Accepted only from trusted regional farms in Uttar Pradesh. Tested for oil content, moisture, and zero impurities before processing.

Step 02

Cold Kacchi Ghani Crush

Slow mechanical extraction keeps temperature below 45°C, ensuring natural allylisothiocyanate (sharp jhaanjh) is preserved.

Step 03

Cotton Filtration

Zero chemical clearing agents or bleaching clay. The fresh oil settles naturally and passes through micro-cotton cloth.

Step 04

Airtight Packing

Hygienically filled and sealed in food-grade virgin containers at Lambhua, keeping purity and aroma locked until opened in your kitchen.
